# Swarangan website — admin guide

A plain guide to looking after the website. No technical knowledge needed.

**Sign in:** go to **www.swarangan.sg/admin** and enter your email and password.
Everything you save appears on the website straight away.

---

## First time only

On the **Dashboard** you may see a card that says **"Import the website's
content"**. Click it once. It copies all the current text, testimonials, photos
and videos into the editors, exactly as written. The website looks the same
afterwards.

---

## Everyday tasks

### Reply to an enquiry

1. **Enquiries** tab. New messages are listed first.
2. Click **Reply to …** to answer by email, or tap the phone number to call.
3. Change the status to **Replied**. Use **Archived** to tidy away old ones.

If email notifications are set up, each new enquiry also arrives in your inbox.

### Add or update a student

1. **Students** tab → **Add student**.
2. Fill in the name, class, level, where they learn and the date they joined.
3. When a student stops, change their status to **Paused** or **Left** rather
   than deleting them — that keeps the dashboard charts accurate.

The student list is private and never appears on the website.

### Add a testimonial

**Testimonials** → **Add testimonial** → enter the name and the words → **Save**.
Leave an empty line between paragraphs. Use the arrows to change the order and
the eye button to hide one without deleting it.

### Add photos to the gallery

1. **Gallery** tab. To start a new album, click **Add album** and give it a
   name.
2. In the album, click **Add photos** and choose one or more photos. They are
   resized automatically.
3. Click the pencil on a photo to add a **caption** (shown on the website) and a
   short **description** (read aloud to visitors who cannot see the photo).

### Change Tanuja's photo

**Images** → choose a new photo → drag the focus point to where the face is →
**Save**. **Reset** goes back to the original photo.

### Change page text

**Page text** for the home page sections, **Classes & fees** for the class
descriptions. Click the pencil, edit, **Save**.

### Publish fees and timings

**Classes & fees** → **Add fee plan**. Plans stay hidden until you click the eye
button to show them. The fees section appears on the Classes page once at
least one plan is shown.

### Videos and Instagram

**Social media** tab:

- **Videos**: paste a YouTube link and a title. **Featured** videos appear
  first.
- **Instagram posts and reels**: on Instagram, open the post → **Share** →
  **Copy link**, then paste it here.

### Contact details, and hiding the phone or WhatsApp

**Settings**: phone, email, address, social media links, and the short phrases
used across the site. The switches at the top of each group hide the phone
number, the WhatsApp buttons, or a whole section of the Social Presence page.
Remember to click **Save settings**.

---

## Once a month: take a backup

**Backup** tab → type your password → **Download backup**. Keep the file
somewhere private (it contains students' contact details).

---

## If something is not right

| What you see                           | What to do                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Forgot your password                   | On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?** and follow the email.                                    |
| "Too many attempts"                    | Wait the few minutes it says, then try again carefully.                                                       |
| An admin page says it could not load   | Click **Try again**. If it keeps failing, ask the person who set up the site to check Supabase.               |
| Dashboard warns "No ping for …"        | The database keep-awake check has stopped. Ask the person who set up the site (SETUP.md step 9).              |
| A change is not showing on the website | Refresh the page. Check the item is not hidden (eye button).                                                  |
| Deleted something by mistake           | Deletions cannot be undone in the admin. A recent backup can restore it — ask for help with SETUP.md step 12. |
